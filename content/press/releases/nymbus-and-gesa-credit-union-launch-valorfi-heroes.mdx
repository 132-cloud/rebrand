**JACKSONVILLE, FL — March 6, 2025** - [Nymbus](/), a full-stack banking platform for U.S. banks and credit unions, is proud to announce the public launch of [ValorFI Heroes](https://valorfiheroes.com) in collaboration with Gesa Credit Union. This fully digital, national banking platform is specifically designed to serve multiple member verticals, starting with law enforcement officers, healthcare workers, first responders, educators, veterans, and those who support them through purpose-driven banking and charitable giving opportunities.

“This is a momentous occasion for Gesa Credit Union,” said Don Miller, President and CEO representing ValorFI Heroes. “I am deeply proud of our dedicated team that brought this vision to life and their commitment over the last year with Nymbus. Together, we’re delivering an innovative solution to market—better meeting the evolving needs of people through technological advancements—and making an incredible impact on these hero groups that really shows the power of giving in numbers.”

The ValorFI Heroes difference is amplified with the third party integration of [Spiral](https://www.spiral.us/products) into the Nymbus banking platform. This charitable giving provider gives ValorFI Heroes and its members multiple ways to donate to 501(c)(3) charities related to affinity groups and track individual and collective contributions natively within the Nymbus online banking experience.

“It was incredibly important to build a system that puts our members in control of when, where, and how they give. Transparency and flexibility were essential,” said Andrew Chung, CIO representing ValorFI Heroes. “The ease of integrations in the Nymbus platform allows us to partner with third parties like Spiral and deliver new, bespoke experiences to our members.”

Thanks to Nymbus’s dynamic theming capabilities, ValorFI Heroes provides a unique affinity group experience across mobile and online banking platforms. This allows branding and messaging flexibility across all member segments and the ability to issue debit cards by affinity group to promote top-of-wallet usage.

“Nymbus is proud to partner with Gesa Credit Union to deliver a bespoke digital banking experience tailored to the unique needs of everyday heroes in our shared communities,” said Nymbus Executive Chairman and CEO Jeffrey Kendall. “By delivering a comprehensive suite of banking solutions customized to their preferences, we are able to honor those who serve our communities while helping Gesa foster deeper, more personalized relationships with its members, who expect a high degree of flexibility and responsiveness from a financial institution.”

Community heroes and their supporters can learn more about ValorFI Heroes and become a member at [valorfiheroes.com](https://valorfiheroes.com).

### About Gesa Credit Union

Gesa Credit Union is one of Washington’s largest credit unions, with over $5.5 billion in assets and over 292,000 members strong. Gesa is a full-service financial institution that offers a complete array of consumer, mortgage, and business products and services. Headquartered in Richland, Washington, Gesa operates 32 branches, loan centers, full-service student-operated campus branches at Washington State University, and 15 student-operated high school branches across Washington state. The Gesa Community Foundation, established in 2022, further supports Gesa’s ongoing commitment to local communities and charitable giving, which includes support for schools, Local Heroes groups, and community organizations through its Affinity Debit Card Program, as well as through its annual youth scholarships, and free financial and educational resources available to members and the general public. For more information, visit [gesa.com](https://gesa.com).

### About Nymbus

Nymbus delivers a cloud-based, highly extensible, full-stack banking platform, empowering community banks and credit unions to accelerate their growth and market positioning. Founded in 2015, Nymbus modernizes legacy core systems that support both brick-and-mortar operations and digital-first institutions, and facilitates the launch of vertical banking strategies or subsidiary brands with a sidecar Core alternative. The Nymbus Banking Platform solution delivers the technology, people, and processes to scale as your financial institution grows. For more information, visit [www.nymbus.com](/).
