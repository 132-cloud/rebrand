**JACKSONVILLE, FL – March 15, 2022** – [NYMBUS®](/), a leading provider of banking technology solutions, today announced the appointment of Stuart Muirhead to its Industry Advisory Board.
Encompassing over a dozen cross-industry leaders, the combined team of Advisors provide diverse layers of guidance as Nymbus continues to advance as the preferred fintech partner for growth.
{:.mb-3\}
“Stuart has decades of experience leading business transformation within the financial market, notably by bridging technology with innovative banking approaches to open up new opportunities for his clients,” said Jeffery Kendall, Chairman and CEO of Nymbus. “His track record and passion are a natural fit and excellent addition to our Advisory Board and Nymbus’ partner institutions who are seeking out fresh and impactful digital strategies to grow their businesses.”
{:.mb-3\}
Currently as Managing Director and Global Head of Wholesale Middle Office at HSBC Holdings, Stuart leads Middle Office Operations for HSBC's investment bank and is responsible for various financial crime, legal, and regulatory controls globally. He also runs and manages the onboarding and maintenance of clients to HSBC's investment bank.
{:.mb-3\}
Stuart was born and raised in South Africa and attended University in the USA where he played Division 1 soccer and also ran track. After serving 9 years in the British Army, Stuart worked at Goldman Sachs and Stryker Europe before joining HSBC in 2012. Prior to his current role, he most recently served as Managing Director and Head of Client Operations for GBM Business Services at the company. ​
 {:.mb-4\}
The Nymbus Industry Advisory Board’s esteemed list of members bring expertise for guiding brands, banks, fintechs and credit unions to leverage Nymbus’ unique innovation model, which quickly creates new digital banking products, services and experiences for sustainable business growth.
For more information, please visit [nymbus.com](/).
