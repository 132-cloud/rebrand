**JACKSONVILLE, FL – March 6, 2024** – [NYMBUS®](/), a premier provider of cloud-based financial technology solutions, is thrilled to announce its recognition as one of "[America’s Best Startup Employers 2024](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rachelpeachman/2024/03/05/meet-americas-best-startup-employers-2024/)" by [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/). This accolade underscores Nymbus' commitment to excellence in leadership, forward-thinking strategies, and dynamic workplace culture, establishing the company as a top destination for talent in the fintech sector.

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau's Business Formation Statistics](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/index.html), 2023 marked a record-breaking year for startup formations in the United States, with nearly 5.5 million businesses launched. Amidst this unprecedented surge, Nymbus distinguished itself by navigating the challenges of scaling and securing critical funding and prioritizing the well-being and engagement of its team members.

Under the strategic direction of Chairman and CEO Jeffery Kendall, Nymbus has redefined the financial technology landscape. By delivering a state-of-the-art, cloud-based core platform, and solutions for modernization to banks and credit unions, Nymbus is at the forefront of facilitating growth and fostering agility. This approach enables financial institutions to navigate the evolving market more quickly and efficiently.

"This recognition reflects our entire team's dedication and tireless efforts,” Kendall proudly stated. “It highlights our commitment to offering top-tier solutions to our clients and cultivating a supportive and thriving work environment for our staff, underlining our collective success."

Crina Pupaza, Nymbus' Chief People Officer, emphasized the company's unwavering dedication to its workforce. "Our steadfast commitment to upskilling our employees, diversity, outstanding benefits, and workplace flexibility has been instrumental in our success,” she said. We firmly believe in nurturing our team's professional and personal growth, which propels our company forward."

The Forbes accolade solidifies Nymbus' leadership in fintech and enhances its attractiveness to elite talent, emphasizing its role in fostering a fulfilling work environment. As Nymbus continues to shape the future of financial services, attracting and retaining professionals who share its vision is paramount to setting new standards of excellence in the industry.

For more information about Nymbus and its award-winning approach to banking technology, visit [www.nymbus.com](/).
