**JACKSONVILLE, FL – March 18, 2025** – [Nymbus](/), a full-stack banking platform for U.S. banks and credit unions, is thrilled to announce that Chief People Officer Crina Pupaza has been named in the Workplace Culture Leader category of Ragan Communication’s 2025 list of [Top Women in HR](https://www.ragan.com/awards/top-women-hr-awards/2024/winners/).

The awards celebrate the women who champion their organizations' employees and have worked to ensure the highest quality, productivity, employee health, and morale. Pupaza, who has also been recognized [on Ragan’s website](https://www.ragan.com/awards/top-women-hr-awards/2024/winners/), was chosen from a large pool of entries for her impressive work building Nymbus’ People and Culture team.

“Crina Pupaza set herself apart from an outstanding field of entrants,” said Brendan Gannon, Senior Marketing Manager for Awards Programs at Ragan Communications. “Her work was exceptional and displayed innovative strategies for achieving success.”

Pupaza champions continuous learning and skills development, establishing programs and workshops supporting employee career growth. As co-chair of the company’s Women’s Empowerment Network, she is also dedicated to uplifting employees through gender equity initiatives that educate, mentor, and empower women in the workplace.

Her efforts have helped create an inclusive environment where all voices are heard, most recently earning Nymbus recognition by Forbes as one of [America’s Best Startup Employers](https://www.forbes.com/lists/americas-best-startup-employers/) for the second consecutive year.

“Crina’s efforts to bring people together not only encourage collaboration, but strengthen our team’s culture of innovation, respect, and overall excellence,” said Jeffery Kendall, CEO and Chairman of Nymbus. “She leads with empathy, and ensures our team has the resources and support needed to continuously deliver best-in-class technology and services for financial institutions across the U.S. We’re proud to honor her latest achievement.”

### About Nymbus

Nymbus delivers a cloud-based, highly extensible, full-stack banking platform, empowering community banks and credit unions to accelerate their growth and market positioning. Founded in 2015, Nymbus modernizes legacy core systems that support both brick-and-mortar operations and digital-first institutions, and facilitates the launch of vertical banking strategies or subsidiary brands with a sidecar Core alternative. The Nymbus Banking Platform solution delivers the technology, people, and processes to scale as your financial institution grows. For more information, visit [nymbus.com](/).
